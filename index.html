          How to play

          1. 🤫 Secret word. Everyone privately peeks at the same secret word — except the imposter, who gets nothing (or a decoy!).
          2. 🗣️ One-word clues. In order, each player says one word related to the secret word. The imposter must bluff.
          3. 🔎 Discuss & vote. Argue about who sounded fake, then vote secretly on one phone.
          4. 🚨 The twist. If the imposter is caught, they can still steal points by guessing the secret word!
          Scoring Imposter escapes the vote: +3 · Crew catches an imposter: +1 each · Caught imposter guesses the word: +2
